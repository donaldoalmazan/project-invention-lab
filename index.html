Toolkit overview

A friendly guide through the engineering design loop.

Each phase of the process comes with focused AI prompts that keep students on track: define, brainstorm, then prototype.

①
Define the Problem
Build empathy, clarify user needs, and choose a problem worth solving before touching any hardware.
②
Brainstorm Ideas
Generate lots of directions quickly, then use AI to filter for promising concepts and project scope.
③
Prototype Solutions
Translate ideas into circuits, code, and testable physical prototypes using Arduino or Raspberry Pi.

What the Lab helps you do

Move from vague ideas to clearly scoped prototypes with three focused toolsets.

01
Define the Problem
Build empathy for your user’s needs.
Insight Gathering: Analyze your target audience, highlight potential problem areas and user frustrations.
Needs Analysis Tool: Identify and prioritize the needs of your target user, ensuring your solution makes a meaningful impact.
User Research: Generate questions to ask your target user base to dig deeper.
