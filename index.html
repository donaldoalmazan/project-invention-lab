Product Invention Lab is an AI toolkit that walks students through the product design cycle — from defining a problem to prototyping with Arduino or Raspberry Pi.

Whether you're new to hardware or mentoring a classroom of makers, Product Invention Lab keeps every step focused on real user needs — not just cool gadgets.
